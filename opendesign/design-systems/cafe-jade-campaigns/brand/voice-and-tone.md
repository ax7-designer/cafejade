# Voice and tone

- Warm, local and confident. Café Jade invites; it does not shout at the guest.
- Spanish is primary, with correct accents and natural Mexican phrasing.
- Use one clear action per slide. Do not print the WhatsApp number in artwork.
- Prefer “reserva desde el perfil”, “vive la final” and “te esperamos”.
- Avoid generic hype, excessive exclamation marks, football clichés and aggressive rivalry.
- Country names and key event labels may use uppercase for scanning.
