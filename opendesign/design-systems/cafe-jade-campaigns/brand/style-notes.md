# Visual foundations

The production brand uses deep jade, warm cream and muted gold. Campaign layouts keep the official logo visible, use square corners, subtle paper grain and a clear vertical reading order.

Typography extracted from the supplied reference:

- Condensed, heavy uppercase for teams, date and score-like information. Translation: Barlow Condensed 800–900.
- Dry-brush handwritten emphasis for the emotional phrase only. Translation: Knewave, kept to one short line.
- Neutral sans serif for practical information. Translation: DM Sans 500–700.

The reference’s athlete cutouts, torn-paper framing and central versus axis are treated as category conventions, then rebuilt asymmetrically for Café Jade. Generated subjects must be centered, fully visible above the knees, isolated against a flat chroma background and contain no text, logos or watermarks.
