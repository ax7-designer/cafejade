## Nuestra Historia

Cafe Jade nace como un espacio para disfrutar la mesa, el cafe y la cultura local con una mirada cuidada. Aqui la cocina, la hospitalidad y el ambiente se unen para crear una experiencia calida, tradicional y distinta.

A Meeting Place in Palenque

Cafe Jade was created as a place to enjoy food, coffee, and local culture with thoughtful attention to detail. Here, cuisine, hospitality, and atmosphere come together in a warm, traditional, and distinctive experience.
